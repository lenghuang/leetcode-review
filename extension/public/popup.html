Syncing Process

Follow these steps to sync your submissions:

  1. Navigate to your LeetCode submissions page.
  2. Click the "Start Syncing" button in this popup.
  3. The extension will fetch your recent submissions.
  4. Submissions will be processed and added to your Recode AI account.

Note: Syncing may take some time depending on the number of submissions.
